# 00-INBOX

Raw fragments awaiting triage. The single drop point for new captures, transcripts, ideas, and anything that doesn't yet have a home.

---

## How it works

1. **Capture freely.** Drop notes, transcripts, links, half-formed ideas. Don't worry about structure. If it's worth not losing, it goes here.
2. **Trigger triage when full.** Say `triage inbox` to your agent. Usually weekly, or whenever the inbox gets noisy enough to feel like cognitive overhead.
3. **Triage produces a record.** The agent reads everything new, clusters related fragments, proposes destinations and cross-references, and writes a triage doc (`_triage-YYYY-MM-DD.md`).
4. **Approve, then execute.** You review the proposed moves. The agent files everything on your approval. Triage docs archive to `999-ARCHIVE/`.
5. **Inbox empties after each pass.** That's the discipline. An inbox with old fragments in it is just another folder.

---

## What belongs here

- Raw thoughts and captures.
- Transcripts of calls, podcasts, or working sessions awaiting harvest.
- Article drafts before they have a home.
- Links worth saving with a one-line note on why.
- "I might want to do X" fragments before they earn a proposal.

## What doesn't belong here

- Decisions in progress (those go to `30-PROPOSALS/`).
- Active work (that lives in `ROADMAP.md` or its track folder).
- Reference material with a clear destination (file it directly).
