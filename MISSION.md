# Mission

*The narrative spine of this operation. Where we are, where we're going, the principles guiding the work. Read after operator-profile and before ROADMAP. Update when the underlying thesis shifts, not when tactics change.*

---

## 1. What we do

One or two paragraphs on the actual work. Not the marketing version. The honest version, written so a new team member or a fresh agent understands the shape of the operation in 30 seconds.

## 2. Who it's for

Refer to [[operator-profile]] for the canonical answer. Add nuance here if useful: edge cases, who we explicitly don't serve, current trigger events that make someone the right fit.

## 3. The offer

What we sell or deliver. The shape of the engagement, the deliverable, the pricing model. Detail enough that an agent helping you draft sales material or partnership notes pulls from this rather than guessing.

## 4. How the work gets done

The operating model. Tools, throughput multipliers, team shape, what makes this practice economically viable. The factory pattern.

## 6. The bet

The thesis. What we believe about the market, the customer, the technology, or the moment that justifies this operation existing in this shape. The thing that, if we're wrong about it, the strategy doesn't work.

## 7. Sessions framing

How working sessions in this vault are structured. How handoffs happen between humans and agents. What "good" looks like at the end of a session.

## 8. Design principles

The portable rules that shape decisions when no proposal is open and no roadmap item directly applies. Examples:

- Prefer composable pieces over monolithic add-ons.

- Capture before deciding; decide before building.
